# Resposta pra Apple App Review (Guideline 2.1 - Information Needed)

Colar no Resolution Center ("Responda à equipe de revisão de apps") no reenvio.

---

Hello, thank you for reviewing our app. Please find the answers below.

**1. Who is your target audience?**
Members (associates) of vehicle protection associations and fleet-tracking companies in Brazil who already have a GPS tracking device installed in their vehicle. This is a B2B2C companion app — not a general-consumer app.

**2. Is this app restricted to specific users? If so, which users?**
Yes. The app is exclusively for associates/clients of a vehicle protection association or tracking company that already uses our platform. Only users with an active membership and an installed tracking device can use it. It is not intended for the general public.

**3. How do users obtain an account?**
Accounts are created by the association/company (our business customer) in their management dashboard. The associate receives their login credentials (Brazilian CPF number + password) directly from their association. Users do NOT self-register inside the app — there is no sign-up screen. They only log in with the credentials provided to them.

**4. If users create an account, are there fees involved?**
No. The app is free and contains no in-app purchases, subscriptions, or payments. Any membership or service fee is handled entirely offline by the user's association, outside the app.

**5. Must users purchase and install external hardware in order to utilize the app?**
Yes. A GPS tracking device must already be installed in the user's vehicle by their association/company. The app only displays the real-time location reported by that device — it does not sell, ship, or configure any hardware.

**6. If so, what is the purchase price of the device and services?**
The tracking device and service are provided and priced by each vehicle protection association (our B2B customers), entirely outside the app. Pricing varies per association and is never sold or charged through the app or by us via the app.

**7. Is it a one time fee or a subscription based model?**
The membership/service model is defined by each association (typically a monthly membership) and is handled 100% outside the app. The app has no payment, subscription, or purchase mechanism of any kind.

**Demo account for review:** the CPF and password already provided in App Review Information (kept in 1Password, item "conta demo de revisao das lojas" — never in the repository). After logging in, the map shows the real-time satellite location of the vehicle linked to this account.

Please let us know if you need any further information. Thank you.
